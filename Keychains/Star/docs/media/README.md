# Star media

Real footage supplied by Aloe, split into silent GIFs.

| Clip | Source interval | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charging and tilt | 0.0–6.5 s | Green eyes, charging bubbles, rotation and tilt |
| Touch reactions | 6.5–11.6 s | Finger interaction and eye reactions |
| Battery readout | 11.6–16.1 s | Liquid readout while charging |
| Shake and dizzy | 16.1–21.9 s | Shake reaction, spiral pupils and recovery |

The GIFs are displayed in the [Star gallery](../../README.md#in-motion).
The low-battery and neutral photos show Star's red, tired expression and its
regular purple eyes in the [personality gallery](../../README.md#a-little-personality).

Clips were cropped around the keychain and converted from HLG to SDR. The GIFs
are 320 × 400 at 12 FPS and cannot contain audio. Source recordings and MP4
copies are not included in the repository.

Captions describe the footage, not proof of charge completion.
Generated firmware previews live separately in [animations](../animations/).
